NEXUS shows your whole infrastructure as one map, and every incident is now
investigated automatically.

### New

- **NEXUS:** an interactive map of your servers, services, websites and
  pages, with their dependencies, health, active incidents and impact ("what
  falls over if this fails"). The map updates live, and there is a list view
  for keyboard users.
- **NEXUS 3D:** the same map as a 3D world you can rotate.
  - Servers are towers, services are rings, and websites orbit their server.
  - During an incident a pulse runs along the connections.
  - The live flows show measured load, not real traffic.
- **Root cause analysis (NEXUS Intelligence):** every incident is
  investigated automatically with fixed rules, on every plan.
  - You get the most likely explanation with a confidence, evidence for and
    against, alternatives and what is still uncertain.
  - "Cause not determined" is a valid outcome.
- **AI interpretation** of that investigation, on **Pro and Premium**. The AI
  can only refer to evidence ServerScope supplied; unknown references are
  removed and counted.
- **Globe** (under NEXUS): your main location and probes on a globe, with the
  status of each location. A probe with no outage to confirm shows
  "Standby".
- **Heartbeat on the dashboard:** a live ECG line per server.
  - The pace follows the CPU, and the shape shows the rhythm: steady,
    irregular, chaos, flat (offline) or at rest.
  - The rhythm, the reason and the figures are also shown as text.
- **Skyline** (under NEXUS): your servers as a city at night. The height is
  the CPU, the windows are services and the roof lights are websites.
- **Status badges** for your README or website: `/status/{slug}/badge.svg`,
  also per component, with a light or dark theme and in English or Dutch.
- **Status page:** an optional 3D header that only uses public data.

### Changed

- AI incident analysis is now only part of **Pro and Premium**. Earlier
  analyses stay visible.
