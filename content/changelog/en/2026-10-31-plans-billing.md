You can now sign up for ServerScope yourself and choose a plan: Free, Pro or
Premium.

### New

- **The Free, Pro and Premium plans.** Choose a plan on the pricing page and
  sign up with it.
- **Sign-up with e-mail verification.** You accept the terms and the privacy
  statement when you sign up.
- **Team invitations:** the recipient accepts the invitation themselves, the
  link is valid for 7 days, and you can revoke or resend an invitation.
- **Escalation contacts** per team (up to 5).
- **Payments through Mollie:** a subscription, cancelling at the end of the
  period, 7 days of grace after a failed payment, and invoices with
  sequential numbers and VAT.
- A **Plan and billing** page with your plan and your invoices.

### Free plan: new limits

| Limit | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Servers | 3 | 1 |
| Domains | 5 | 3 |
| Pages per website | 25 | 15 |
| Status pages | 1 | 1 |
| Check interval | 5 min | 5 min |
| History | 30 days | 14 days |

Free has no API tokens and no webhooks. Pro and Premium are unchanged.

### Good to know

- Domain names are now unique per team instead of across the whole platform.
- A team above the new limits keeps all its data, but cannot add anything.
