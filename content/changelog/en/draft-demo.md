Want to look around first? At [serverscope.nl/demo](https://serverscope.nl/demo)
you see ServerScope with sample data, without an account.

### New

- **A public demo** with sample servers, websites, incidents, deployments,
  logs and scheduled jobs, which always looks up to date.
- Click around everywhere: the dashboard, NEXUS, incidents with the Time
  Machine and the root cause analysis, reports and logs.
- The home page and the pricing page have a **Try the demo** button.

### Good to know

- The demo is read-only: you cannot add or change anything.
- The demo does nothing outside: no checks, e-mails or notifications go out.
- Everything you see is sample data, not real servers or websites.
